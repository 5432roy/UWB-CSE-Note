#### Imperical Model of Research
1. Induction - looking for cases to make a rule from; place to start.
2. Deduction - go from rule to individual cases (generalize).
3. Test - verify; tweak; iterate.

### DIKW
Data, Information, Knowledge, and Wisdom
- Information: Who, What, When, Where
- Knowledge: Why
- Wisdom: Understanding

### Different Ways of Doing Research
- Observation
    - Ethnographic studies
- Coorelation Studies

- Classic Research
    - Quasi experimental designs

### When Researches Talk (Ideas)

- How much bias introduced into the process
- Training?

### Critical Topics to Think About for your Research

- Who will be mentor
